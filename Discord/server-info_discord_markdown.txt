- **Sells:** Weapons & ammo (civilian tier), attachments, civilian clothing, medical supplies, base building materials, vehicles, blank & master keys

## 🚁 Airdrops & KeyRooms

**Airdrops**
- Server-wide alert when incoming · Transport plane visible in the sky · Smoke flare marks landing site

**KeyRooms**
- Named vaults: **KMUC** · **Temple** · **Crypt**
- 🔘 **Grey Keys** → secure rooms in major Cities

- Doors auto-lock after a set time

## ☢️ Survival Systems

**Radiation (Terje Radiation)**
- Full **6-piece NBC suit** required (Gas Mask is the most critical piece — 41.67% of total protection)
- Clothing & inventory items accumulate contamination — decontaminate before storing
- Buildable **Radiation Decontamination Shower** available at base

**Stamina (Inedia)**
- **Two bars:** White (short-burst sprint stamina) + Yellow (long-term endurance — affected by carried weight & terrain)
- **Overload threshold:** 105 kg · Energy drinks provide temporary recovery

**Psionic Zones**
- Psychoactive hazard areas causing mental health deterioration · **PSI Helmet** required for protection

**Medicine (Terje Medicine)**
- Three forms: Tablets (multi-dose) · Ampoules (require syringe) · Injectors (single-use disposable)
- Three tiers: **Weak / Normal / Strong** — match treatment tier to illness severity
- ⚠️ Overdose system — do not stack the same medicine · Drinking water slows overdose progression

**Armor — 5 Tiers**
🟤 T1 Load-bearing only · 🟡 T2 Soft armor (handgun-rated) · 🟠 T3 Light plate (rifle-rated) · 🔴 T4 Heavy plate (AP rounds) · 🟥 T5 Full military coverage

## 🟢 Beginner Tips
1. **Do the Orientation Quest first** — auto-assigned on first spawn, reward: **500 Hryvnia**
2. **Get a full NBC suit before entering any radiation zone** — one wrong zone visit will kill you without all 6 pieces
3. **Press `B`** while hovering an item to highlight compatible items (🟡 yellow) and same-type items (🟠 orange)
4. **Always lock your vehicle** — lockpicking exists (40% success over 2 minutes) and it will be attempted
5. **Find Skill Books** in the world for burst XP — prioritize Athletics and Immunity early
