    Welcome to the Survivatorium!¶

    To ensure this community remains a welcoming, safe, and enjoyable place for everyone, you are required to read and agree to the following rules. Failure to adhere to these rules will result in consequences at the moderation team's discretion.

    1. Be Respectful and Inclusive¶

    Our primary goal is to maintain a community free from toxicity.

    • Zero Tolerance for Hate: We have a zero-tolerance policy for any form of racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, bigotry, or hate speech.
    • No Harassment or Bullying: Do not engage in personal attacks, insults, targeted harassment, or any form of bullying.
    • No Politics or Religion: To avoid conflict, all discussions related to real-world politics and religion are strictly prohibited.
    • Respect Everyone: Treat all members, including the moderation staff, with respect. Disagreements are fine, but personal insults are not.

    2. No Spamming or Channel Abuse¶

    Keep our channels clean and on-topic.

    • Use Correct Channels: Post your content in the designated channels. (e.g., memes in #memes, looking-for-group posts in #lfg).
    • No Spam: Do not flood the chat with repetitive messages, large blocks of text, excessive images, or GIFs.
    • No Mention Abuse: Do not randomly @mention users, roles, or staff. Use of @everyone and @here is reserved for announcements.
    • No "Rant" Posts: We understand frustration, but excessive or aggressive rants are not productive. Please use appropriate channels to provide constructive feedback.

    3. Keep Content Safe For Work (SFW)¶

    This is a kid-safe, SFW community.

    • No NSFW/Shocking Content: Do not post, link, or stream any content that is pornographic, graphically violent, gory, or otherwise obscene.
    • This applies to all media: text, images, GIFs, videos, links, and profile pictures/banners.
    • NSFW Designated Channels: The only exception is in channels explicitly marked as [NSFW]. If no such channel exists, all content must be SFW.

    4. Protect Community Security and Privacy¶

    Your safety and the safety of others is paramount.

    • No Doxxing: You are forbidden from posting any real-life, personal, or identifying information about any other member (e.g., real name, address, phone number, photos). This will result in an immediate and permanent ban.
    • No Malicious Content: Do not post or share any files, executables, or links that contain viruses, malware, phishing scams, or other harmful software.

    5. No Advertising or Self-Promotion¶

    This community is not your personal billboard.

    • No Unsolicited Advertising: Do not advertise other Discord servers, websites, or social media accounts (Twitch, YouTube, etc.) without explicit permission from a moderator.
    • This includes DMs: Advertising to members via Direct Messages is also forbidden and will result in a ban.

    6. General Gaming and Community Conduct¶

    • No Cheating: Do not discuss, promote, or distribute cheats, hacks, exploits, or emulators for any game.
    • Follow Voice Chat (VC) Etiquette: When in a voice channel, do not mic spam, play loud music, or have excessive background noise.
    • One Account Per User: We only allow one account per user. Using alternate accounts to bypass mutes or bans will result in a permanent ban for all associated accounts.

    7. Adhere to Discord TOS and Staff Instructions¶

    • Follow Discord TOS: All members are expected to follow the Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines at all times.
    • Respect Staff: Listen to and respect the instructions from the Moderator team. Do not argue with moderation decisions in public channels. If you have an issue, please contact a moderator privately.
