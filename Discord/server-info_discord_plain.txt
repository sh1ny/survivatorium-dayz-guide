☢️  SURVIVAL SYSTEMS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
RADIATION
▸ Full 6-piece NBC suit required — Gas Mask is the most critical (41.67% of total protection)
▸ Clothing & inventory items accumulate contamination — decontaminate before storing
▸ Buildable Radiation Decontamination Shower available at base

STAMINA
▸ Two bars: White (sprint) + Yellow (long-term endurance — affected by weight & terrain)
▸ Overload threshold: 105 kg · Energy drinks provide temporary recovery

PSIONIC ZONES
▸ Psychoactive hazard areas — cause mental health deterioration
▸ PSI Helmet required for protection

MEDICINE
▸ Forms  : Tablets (multi-dose) · Ampoules (require syringe) · Injectors (single-use)
▸ Tiers  : Weak / Normal / Strong — match tier to illness severity
▸ ⚠ Overdose system — do not stack the same medicines

ARMOR — 5 TIERS
▸ T1 🟤 Load-bearing only (no armor)
▸ T2 🟡 Soft armor — handgun rated
▸ T3 🟠 Light plate carrier — rifle rated (5.56 / 7.62)
▸ T4 🔴 Heavy plate carrier — AP round rated
▸ T5 🟥 Full military coverage

🟢  BEGINNER TIPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Do the Orientation Quest first — auto-assigned on spawn, reward: 500 Hryvnia
2. Get a full NBC suit before entering any radiation zone — all 6 pieces needed, no shortcuts
3. Press B while hovering an item to highlight compatible (yellow) and same-type (orange) items
4. Lock your vehicle every time — lockpicking exists and it will be attempted on you
5. Hunt for Skill Books in the world — prioritize Athletics and Immunity early
